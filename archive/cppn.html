How it works›

Every image here is a neural network. A Compositional Pattern Producing Network (CPPN) maps each pixel's coordinates (x, y, radius) plus a latent vector (α, β, γ) to an RGB color. It uses sinusoidal and Gaussian activations (the SIREN idea) instead of plain tanh — periodic activations are what give the network its crisp, intricate structure. There is no image file until the network computes one, pixel by pixel. Nothing is drawn, sampled, or pulled from a dataset. This art cannot exist without the network.


It's not just generated — it's evolved. Every artwork has a reproducible seed, and you can even turn any word into art (Name Art renders your typed letters filled with the network's texture, seeded by the word — so your name always makes the same piece). Mutate perturbs the weights to explore nearby variations. Breed crosses two artworks and opens a generation grid — click the offspring you like and it breeds a new generation from there, over and over. Human-guided neuro-evolution, the same idea behind Picbreeder and NEAT. Your taste is the fitness function.


Because the image is a continuous function of coordinates, you can zoom in forever and new detail keeps emerging — there is no underlying resolution, so Export HD can render it at any size. Symmetry folds the coordinate field into kaleidoscopes. Audio React lets your microphone drive the latent vector (bass→α, mids→β, treble→γ). Morph smoothly interpolates the network's weights between two artworks — a hypnotic transition you can Record as video, alongside the evolving latent animation. Share encodes the exact seed into a link (with a scannable QR code) anyone can reopen.


Built with TensorFlow.js. No server, no API — the network runs entirely in your browser. Created for Hack the Arts 2026.
